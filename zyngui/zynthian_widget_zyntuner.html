zyntuner

Low CPU tuner for western and microtonal scales, and spectrum analyser. In Tuner mode, like a tuner pedal, the output is muted while the tuner is on (with a short fade, no clicks). Bypass it to hear the audio again: it then passes through unchanged. In Spectrum mode the audio always passes. A new tuner starts bypassed: switch Bypass off to tune or analyse. Only the selected mode runs, and nothing runs while bypassed. In Both mode, Bypass switches between the two: tuner (muted) while inline, spectrum (audio passing) while bypassed.

Tuner

Play one note at a time. The nearest note of the selected scale is shown with its octave and its deviation in cents. The needle is left of centre when the note is flat and right when it is sharp. The colour is orange within 15 cents and green when in tune (within 3 cents). The needle shows up to 50 cents: scales with wider steps can be further away, see the cents value. "--" means that no pitch is detected. The selected scale is shown on the left.

Spectrum

32 bars from 20 Hz to 20 kHz (about a third of an octave each), from -80 to 0 dB. 0 dB is a full scale sine wave. Bars rise at once and fall at 40 dB per second. They turn orange above -12 dB and red above -3 dB. Below about 100 Hz each bar covers only one or two analysis points: a steady tone there can read up to 3 dB low.

Controls

  • A4 reference (415 .. 466 Hz): tuning reference, normally 440 Hz
  • Mode: Tuner, Spectrum or Both (Bypass switches between tuner and spectrum)
  • Bypass: stops the tuner or analyser and unmutes the audio
  • Scale: the scale or tuning the notes are compared to (Tuner)

Scales

Western tunings keep A4 at the reference, the others start at the equal tempered C4 of the reference. Historical temperaments and just intonation are in the key of C.

  • 12-TET: standard equal temperament
  • Just (C): 5-limit just intonation
  • Pythagorean (C): pure fifths, Eb to G#
  • Meantone 1/4: quarter-comma meantone, Eb to G#
  • Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti: well temperaments for baroque music
  • 19-TET, 31-TET: meantone-like equal temperaments, with distinct sharps and flats. "+" and "-" are a step above or below
  • 24-TET: quarter tones (arabic maqam). "+" is a quarter tone sharp
  • 53-TET: Turkish makam, close to Pythagorean and just intervals. "+n" / "-n" are steps of a comma
  • 22 Shruti: Indian classical, Sa to Ni4 (1 .. 4: lower to higher variant)
  • Slendro, Pelog: Javanese gamelan (5 equal steps, and a typical 7-note pelog). Notes are numbered as in kepatihan notation. Real gamelans each have their own tuning
  • Thai 7-TET: 7 equal steps
  • Harmonics 8-15: harmonics 8 to 15 of the root
  • Partch 43: Harry Partch's 43-tone just scale, shown as ratios
  • Bohlen-Pierce: 13 equal steps of a tritave (3:1) instead of an octave. The number after the note is the tritave

Tips

The left and right inputs are mixed for the detection and the analysis: signals in opposite phase cancel. The tuner ignores signals below -60 dB.
